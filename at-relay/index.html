Relay plan: Rockfish Gap → US-522

18 legs, 107.7 miles, ~14,700 ft of climbing (minimum estimate) — strict rotation
Leg boundaries use real Skyline Drive parking and trailheads along the A.T., including Riprap Parking (MP 90.0), Blackrock Gap Parking (MP 87.1), Lewis Mountain Campground (MP 57.5), Hawksbill Gap trailhead (MP 45.5), and Little Stony Man Parking (MP 39.0). One exception: segment 6/7 meets at Doyles River Trail Parking (MP 81.1), a roadside pull-off with no facilities where the A.T. is a short walk down the Doyles River Trail rather than a direct crossing — making segment 7 the longest leg at 9.0 miles. Pinefield Gap–Powell Gap is merged to keep the leg count at 18. Runner totals range 16.6–19.2 miles, from A.T. waypoint distance data, rotating in strict sequential order (1→6, 1→6, 1→6). Vertical gain is a floor estimate summed from known uphill steps — legs marked * span wide data gaps — since independent sources put full-section climbing near 23,000 ft, so treat gain as conservative/relative, not trail-exact.
