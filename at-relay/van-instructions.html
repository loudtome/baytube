Van Driving Instructions

Rockfish Gap → US-522 relay · 18 segments · Skyline Drive mileposts
Where a leg boundary is a Skyline Drive road crossing, meet runners at the nearest parking for that milepost (MP). The segment 6/7 exchange is at Doyles River Trail Parking (MP 81.1) — a plain roadside pull-off with no facilities. Loft Mountain (which has a camp store/wayside/restrooms) is passed mid-leg on segment 7. Segments 7/8 and 13/14 exchange at Ivy Creek Overlook (MP 77.6) and Corbin Cabin Parking (MP 37.8) respectively — both replace older boundary points and are described in detail below.
